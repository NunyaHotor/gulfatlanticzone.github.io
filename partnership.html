Partnership Models

Practical, transparent cooperation designed for low-risk market entry and evidence-based growth.

How We Partner

Built for clarity and aligned incentives

We start lean and scale when demand, commercial terms, and operational capacity justify it.

Non-Exclusive Market Development

Sales representation focused on generating and qualifying demand without exclusivity at the outset. Any exclusivity requires separate written agreement and performance evidence.

Commission-Based Facilitation

Commission on eligible sales generated or materially developed by GAZ. Rates, eligibility, exclusions, and payment timing are documented in writing before commissionable activity begins.

Lead Qualification & Quotation Support

Local customer identification, needs assessment, and coordination of formal quotations. Leads are registered; both parties retain references for reconciliation.

Product Promotion

Use of supplier-approved brochures, images, specifications, and where agreed, demonstrations. We do not promise performance beyond confirmed supplier terms.

Path to Distribution

When demand is proven and terms support it, explore wholesale or broader representation arrangements—including selective inventory where appropriate.

Transparent Process

Written lead registration, regular pipeline reviews, and no binding price quotes or delivery promises without supplier confirmation.

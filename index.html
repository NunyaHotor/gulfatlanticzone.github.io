Who We Are

A practical bridge between global innovation and local opportunity

Gulf Atlantic Zone Company Ltd (GAZ) is a Ghanaian company established in 2023. We focus on agricultural value-chain development, international trade, and connecting international businesses with opportunities in Ghana and the wider African market.

Our approach is demand-led: we identify real customer needs, qualify prospects, facilitate supplier–buyer engagement, and build partnerships that grow in response to demonstrated demand.

Vision

To become a trusted bridge between global agricultural innovation and African agricultural markets.

Mission

To connect international agricultural technology providers with African farmers and agribusinesses through market development, trade facilitation, and sustainable partnerships.

Value Proposition

We reduce the distance between suppliers and local market opportunities—understanding needs, developing pipelines, and enabling clear commercial relationships.

What We Do

Core business areas

We develop market opportunities across key segments of the agricultural value chain.

🚜 Machinery & Implements

Market development for tractors, farm implements, spraying equipment, seeding equipment, and related machinery.

🌱 Seeds & Inputs

Connecting producers and suppliers of seeds and agricultural inputs with buyers and commercial partners.

💧 Irrigation & Technology

Irrigation systems, water-management technologies, and practical farm technologies suited to local conditions.

🌾 Agro-Processing

Grain-processing equipment and technologies that support post-harvest handling and value addition.

🌍 Trade & Representation

Supplier–buyer introductions, local market development, product promotion, and commercial cooperation.

📋 Sales Facilitation

Structured outreach, needs assessment, quotation coordination, and pipeline management for qualified opportunities.

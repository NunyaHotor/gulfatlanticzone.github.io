About Gulf Atlantic Zone

Connecting agricultural innovation with African market opportunities since 2023.

Our Story

Built to close the gap between suppliers and buyers

Gulf Atlantic Zone Company Ltd was established in Ghana in 2023 with a clear purpose: to serve as a practical link between modern agricultural technology and the farmers, agribusinesses, and distributors who need it.

Too often, international manufacturers struggle to understand local demand, while local buyers lack reliable access to suitable equipment and inputs. GAZ sits in the middle—identifying needs, qualifying opportunities, and facilitating commercial relationships that can grow on evidence.

We are a registered Ghanaian company (CS139990923 · TIN C0063284650), based in Tema, and focused first on the Ghana market with longer-term interest across West Africa.

Vision

To become a trusted bridge between global agricultural innovation and African agricultural markets.

Mission

To connect international agricultural technology providers with African farmers and agribusinesses through market development, trade facilitation, and partnerships that support productive and sustainable value chains.

Principles

Transparency · Evidence-based planning · Professional handling of leads · Respect for written agreements · Customer needs and after-sales readiness.
